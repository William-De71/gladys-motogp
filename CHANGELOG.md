## [0.1.0] - unreleased

### Added

- The MotoGP World Championship from motogp.com, without an account or a key: the calendar of the MotoGP, Moto2, Moto3 and Bagger World Cup sessions (practice, qualifying, sprint, race, warm up, tests), the results of the races, sprints and qualifying, and the championship standings.
- One Gladys calendar per followed class for every user who enables the integration in "My calendars", with the chosen kinds of sessions, the rest of the season and the next one, in the language of each user.
- Scene triggers "A session starts" (at the start or 15 minutes to 2 hours before), "Results are out" (with the podium and the result of your favorite riders, filterable) and "New championship leader".
- Scene actions "Next session", "Championship standings" and "Last results", with a ready-made sentence.
- Dashboard widgets "Next session" (countdown and the rest of the weekend), "Schedule", "Standings" and "Results", the favorite riders highlighted.
- English, French, German and Spanish everywhere: settings, calendars, widgets, scenes and documentation.
